FAQs

Is it for me?

Our classes are suitable for beginners and experienced athletes. We will help you find the right level for you.

What can I expect?

You can expect a welcoming environment, professional coaching, and workouts adapted to your level.

What is a lesson like?

Each lesson includes a warm-up, instruction, the main workout, and a cool-down.

Can I train with an injury?

Please speak with your coach before training so we can determine whether exercises can safely be adapted.
